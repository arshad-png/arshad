## Competitor profiles (one record per product)

### Takeaway
The layer is crowded but split into four camps: (1) horizontal SMB messaging suites with annual contracts (Podium, Weave, Birdeye, Demandforce), (2) GoHighLevel and med spa re-skins of it (Aesthetix CRM appears to be one; agencies such as Med Spa Magic Marketing say so openly), (3) AI layers owned by or bolted to the booking/EMR platform (Zenoti AI Workforce, Boulevard Beau, Mangomint Mira, PatientNow + Recura, Prospyr, Pabau), and (4) independent voice-AI receptionists (Clara AI, AgentZap, Goodcall, Hey Rosie, Smith.ai, Frontdesk). Only a handful publish prices.
