# Pilot offer

Prepared 2026-11-20. Status: draft for review. This offer has not been shown to anyone. Every price and term is a hypothesis to test and a proposal to approve, not a validated or committed figure.

Internal sections are marked. The customer-facing draft starts at "The offer as a prospect would read it".

## Internal: design principles

1. One job. Every new inquiry gets a fast, consented reply and a follow-up until it books or opts out, and gets counted through to a showed consult.
2. The same configuration for every spa on a package. Differences between packages are features switched on or off, not custom work.
3. Terms that answer the complaints the research documented about incumbents: published prices, month-to-month, no auto-renewing annual contract, cancel without a call, the spa keeps its data.
4. Honest limits printed in the offer. No calendar write-back. No AI voice. No promised number of bookings.
5. Nothing in a package that cannot be delivered on GoHighLevel today without code.

## Internal: what is deliberately left out

| Left out | Reason |
|---|---|
| Writing appointments into the booking system | Gated or absent on the platforms independents use |
| AI voice receptionist | Most contested category, highest compliance cost |
| AI chat that holds a conversation with leads | Adds health-data and accuracy risk before the basic cadence is proven. Could be a later test |
| Instagram and Facebook DM automation | Demand undocumented, 24-hour reply window, more approvals |
| Reactivation blasts to old patient lists | Highest texting risk under the TCPA |
| Sending conversion data back to Meta or Google | Health-data risk, pending legal review |
| Ad management, website work, review requests, social posting | These turn the product back into an agency retainer |

The last row is the hard one. Pilot spas will ask for these. The answer during the pilot is no, logged as a custom request in the scorecard. If CCS wants to sell those services, they are sold separately under the agency, at agency prices, and kept out of the pilot numbers.

## Internal: items that need a decision before this is shown to anyone

1. Overage rate. The draft says $0.02 per segment, only after the spa opts in. That figure is an ESTIMATE set above an estimated cost of about $0.012 to $0.013 and has to be reset once real GoHighLevel rates are read from the agency account.
2. Founding-customer price lock. The draft locks the pilot price for 12 months after the pilot. That is a real commercial commitment.
3. The 30-day money-back guarantee. Also a real commitment.
4. Product name. The research recommends a distinct name so it does not read as an agency-resold CRM. None is proposed here.
5. Legal entity, payment method, and who signs the BAA.
6. Whether Texas, Florida and Washington spas are in or out of the first pilots, pending counsel.

Flagging 1, 2 and 3 loudly: they are written as sensible defaults, and each one costs money if it is wrong.

## The offer as a prospect would read it

### What this is

A 90-day pilot for independent med spas. It makes sure every new inquiry gets a reply within minutes and a follow-up until they book or say stop, and it shows you what happened to every lead.

It sits beside your booking system. You keep the system you have.

### The problem it fixes

A new patient fills out a form at 7pm or calls while your front desk is checking someone out. By the time anyone gets back to them, they've booked somewhere else. You paid for that lead and you never see it again.

### How it works

1. Every form, Meta lead, Google lead and missed call lands in one place.
2. The person gets a text and an email within a few minutes, from your spa, with your booking link.
3. If they don't book, they get a short, polite series of follow-ups over two weeks. It stops the moment they book or reply STOP.
4. Your team sees every open lead in one inbox, with a clock showing how long each has waited.
5. You get a report showing leads, response time, booked and showed, by source.

### Terms

- 90-day pilot, billed monthly, month-to-month. No annual contract and no auto-renewal into one.
- No setup fee during the pilot.
- Cancel any time by email. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month. No call, no buyout.
- 30-day money-back guarantee on the first month.
- If you stay after the pilot, your pilot price holds for 12 months.
- If you reach your included messages, we tell you before anything pauses. Extra messages are $0.02 per segment and only if you say yes in writing.

### What we measure, and how

For the first two weeks we count your leads and response times as they are today, before switching on any automation. That's the baseline. After that, each week you see:

- Leads by source
- Time to first reply
- Share of leads that booked
- Share that showed (Convert and Convert plus)
- Cost per showed consult by source (Convert plus, using the ad spend you give us)

At day 90 you get a before-and-after summary. If the numbers didn't move, you'll see that too.

### What it does not do

- It does not put appointments on your calendar. Leads book through your own booking page, and your team confirms who showed with one tap.
- It does not answer the phone with AI.
- It does not replace your booking, charting or payment system.
- It does not guarantee a number of bookings. It guarantees every lead gets a fast reply and a follow-up.
- It does not include ad management, website changes or social media.

### What works with your booking system

We publish exactly what we can and can't read from each booking platform, and we only list something as working once we've confirmed it in a real account. During the pilot, every platform works through your public booking link. Automatic booked-and-showed updates are added per platform as they're confirmed.

### What we need from you

- About an hour of setup time, and 30 minutes of training for whoever works the inbox.
- Your business legal name and tax ID for texting registration. Carriers require it.
- Access to your website form and your Meta and Google lead forms so we can add the consent wording.
- Someone on your team who marks leads as showed. It takes a few seconds each.
- Two weeks of baseline before go-live.

### Your data

Your leads and their consent records are yours. You can export them at any time, and you get a full export if you cancel. We do not sell or share lead data, and we do not use it to market to your patients. We sign a business associate agreement with you before any lead data flows.

### Consent and texting

Leads only get automated texts if they agreed to them on the form. The consent wording names your spa, says what they'll receive and how often, and tells them to reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help, and that message and data rates may apply. Every text stops as soon as someone replies STOP. No automated texts go out late at night or early in the morning.

## Internal: how this offer gets judged

The offer passes if strangers buy it at a published price, the same configuration works for each of them, and it is still profitable after labour. It fails as a business, even with happy customers, if each sale needs a custom scope or each spa needs hours of unbilled work every month. VALIDATION-SCORECARD.md tracks both.
