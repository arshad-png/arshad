# Validation scorecard

Prepared 2026-11-20. Status: empty template for review. No data has been collected. Empty cells are to be filled as evidence arrives.

Update weekly once Gate 0 is approved. Record counts as well as percentages. With samples this small, 3 of 5 and 60% are not the same strength of evidence.

## 1. Pass and fail criteria

Every threshold is an ASSUMPTION proposed for agreement at Gate 0. Agree them before any evidence comes in and do not move them afterwards. If a threshold turns out to be badly set, record the reason and the change in section 9.

| O1 | Median time to first automated reply | Under 5 minutes | Over 15 minutes | GoHighLevel report |

| O3 | Booked consults per 100 leads | Up at least 20% relative to baseline, in at least 60% of pilots | Up in fewer than 40% of pilots | Baseline against pilot |

O3 is the weakest measurement here. Lead volumes at a single spa over 90 days are small and seasonal, so a 20% change can be noise. Report the raw counts beside every percentage.
